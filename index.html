Key service

AC gas refilling.

AC running but not cooling? It may be low on gas. We check the pressure and refill it so your AC cools properly again.

Pressure low

Signs your AC needs gas

  • It blows air, but the air isn’t cold
  • Ice or frost forms on the copper pipes
  • It runs for hours and the room stays warm
